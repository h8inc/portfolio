KYC. Know your customer, or kill your customer?

Strike says it spends $0 to acquire users, so every “Sign up” that leaves is free demand lost. 6 in 10 Europeans have quit a financial sign-up, most often over the personal-information ask (Signicat, 2026). My estimate for strike.me: only 3 to 9 in 100 people who tap “Sign up” fund within a week.
