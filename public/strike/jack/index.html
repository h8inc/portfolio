Strike · stackers are borrowers in waiting · Nasko Terziev · November 202601 / 15

Strike makes you move your own money, by hand, at every step.

Two markets, two jobs. US: the product is complete, so turn stackers into borrowers. EU: the product is a commodity, so out-convert the bitcoin-only peers and treat borrowing as the long game.

Why it matters. Payday and Bill Pay help keep US users (07, 08). The EU has no equivalent, and spending bitcoin takes six steps (13). So the EU question is how to keep stackers: pay by bank and auto-buy on deposit (12), then spend.

Out of scope: a card, subscriptions, paid rewards (EU interchange is capped at 0.2%), a fight with Revolut or Trade Republic, a Turbo Buy copy, and any model of Strike's P&L.

Borrow · US · sized to the stack06 / 15

Show the borrow number sized to the stack the person already built.

TriggerAfter N months of stacking, not at sign-up. “You've stacked 0.31 ₿ over 7 months. At 50% LTV that's $X available without selling.” N comes from Strike's data (question 5).
Why it worksThe number is the user's, so it reads as information, not a sales pitch. Selling to pay a bill is the alternative; the calculator shows the tax you'd owe on that sale next to the interest.
MetricBorrowers with a first draw within 30 days of the prompt, by months stacked. Guard: credit disclosures already in the US flow.
An optional question, not an askStrike's no-liquidation loan is capped at 50% LTV with a six-month term. Would a small, capped, volatility-proof Boost on recurring buys suit stackers of six months or more? Reg Z disclosures; a quarter or more. Only if Strike wants it.

Verdicts: the stack-sized calculator is a test behind a flag, no licence change. The Boost is a question for Strike, not a recommendation. EU borrowing waits on CCD2 (applies 20 November 2026) or a bank partner. Precedent: Invity shows a loan explainer and calculator before ID (slide 04).
